VELORANTIB (SFT-301 PROGRAMME) - INVESTIGATOR'S BROCHURE, EDITION 4, 15-Jan-2026
Section 7.3  Reference Safety Information (RSI)

The following serious adverse reactions are considered EXPECTED for expedited reporting purposes.
Expected serious adverse reactions (MedDRA preferred terms):
- Upper respiratory tract infection
- Neutropenia
- Alanine aminotransferase increased
- Pruritus

A reaction not listed above, or a listed reaction that is more severe or more specific than described,
is UNEXPECTED and must be assessed for expedited (SUSAR) reporting.
